# THE FOUR PILLARS OF PERSUASIVE DESIGN

## Primary task support

Supports the user in performing the main task. An app must make the desired behavior easier and guide users step by step.

Tailoring, Tunneling, Simulation, Self-direction

---

## System credibility

An app must appear reliable and credible. Users are more likely to believe in the functioning of the app if it appears professionally transparent and expert.

- Trust, 
- Authority, 
- Expertise,
- Transparency

---

## Dialog support

Strengthens the interaction between user and system through feedback, compliments, and rewards. Creates a sense of contact and recognition.

- Suggestion, 
- Reward, 
- Reminder, 
- Similarity

---

## Social support

People are influenced by what others do.  Social comparison, cooperation, and recognition strengthen motivation and perseverance.

- Comparison, 
- Cooperation, 
- Norms, 
- Recognition
